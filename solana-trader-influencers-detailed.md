## Important Warnings & Best Practices

### Scam Awareness
⚠️ **Never share your seed phrase** - Not even with bots
⚠️ **Verify all groups** - Many fake groups exist
⚠️ **Watch for pump & dumps** - Telegram groups can manipulate markets
⚠️ **Check track records** - Past performance doesn't guarantee future results
⚠️ **Start small** - Test with small amounts first

### Risk Management
- Only invest what you can afford to lose
- Diversify across multiple traders
- Set stop-losses
- Monitor regularly
- Don't blindly copy - understand the strategy
- Memecoin trading is extremely high risk

### Due Diligence
- Cross-reference information from multiple sources
- Check wallet history on Solscan
- Verify PnL claims
- Join groups to assess quality before paying
- Ask for proof of past calls
- Use tools like ZachXBT for fraud detection

### Red Flags
❌ Guaranteed returns
❌ Pressure to join quickly
❌ Requests for seed phrases
❌ Unverifiable claims
❌ No transaction history
❌ Anonymous teams asking for money
