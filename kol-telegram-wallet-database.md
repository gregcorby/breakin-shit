## Important Notes & Warnings

### Privacy & Security

⚠️ **Wallet Privacy Concerns:**
- Most successful traders use MULTIPLE wallets
- Public wallet ≠ complete holdings
- KOLs may have dozens of anonymous wallets
- Tracking one wallet doesn't show full picture

⚠️ **Telegram Safety:**
- Verify official channels before joining
- Never share seed phrases in Telegram
- Watch for impersonator accounts
- Official bots won't ask for private keys

⚠️ **Copy Trading Risks:**
- KOLs may dump on followers ("exit liquidity")
- Public wallet trades might be decoys
- Frontrunning is common
- Slippage on large following can kill profits

### Research Limitations

**What's Difficult to Find:**
- Comprehensive list of KOL names + wallets + Telegram in one place
- Mid-tier traders (under 100K) often stay more anonymous
- Many successful traders don't publicly link all their socials
- Wallet addresses change frequently for security

**Why:**
- Privacy and security concerns
- Tax implications
- Front-running prevention
- Regulatory concerns
- Competitive advantage preservation

### Best Practices

✅ **Do:**
- Cross-reference wallet addresses on multiple platforms
- Verify identity through multiple sources
- Use official tracking tools (Arkham, GMGN, Kolscan)
- Set up automated tracking via Telegram bots
- Follow wallets, not just people
- Diversify information sources

❌ **Don't:**
- Blindly copy trades
- Trust wallet addresses from random Telegram messages
- Assume one wallet shows complete holdings
- Share your private keys with any bot
- Join paid groups without thorough vetting
- Ignore red flags (scam history, pump schemes)
